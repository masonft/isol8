    Twin sections

      Not every song works

      isol8 couldn't find two sections that cancel. It needs a passage that was copied and pasted in the producer's session, sample for sample, like a chorus pasted in twice. Songs that were played or sung all the way through, re-recorded repeats, or heavily processed masters won't cancel.

      Try another song. If you know a repeat is in there, open Debug the detection below to see why it was missed.
